Privacy policy

Sumshine · Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Español

In short: the game has no accounts or servers of its own and doesn't collect your data. Your progress is stored only on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google uses to show the ads you choose to watch in exchange for a hint.

Who is responsible

Sumshine is developed by Iván López Muñoz. For any question about this policy or your data, write to sumshine.game@gmail.com.

What is stored on your device

  • The levels you have solved.
  • The moves of a level you left unfinished, so you can pick up where you left off.
  • Your sound and vibration settings.

This data never leaves your phone, and nobody else, the developer included, has access to it. You can delete it in Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Hints can be unlocked by watching an ad, always at your request. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure them and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • Your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred).
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID.
  • Interactions with ads (for example, whether they are viewed or tapped).
  • Diagnostic information, such as SDK errors.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google handles it under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before using your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy → Ads).

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask you to sign up, or for your name, email or any other personal data.
  • It doesn't use analytics or tracking of its own.
  • It doesn't sell or share data with anyone beyond what is described about ads.
  • It doesn't ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or file permissions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Since the developer keeps no data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on their side: your progress is only on your device. For ad data, you can exercise your rights with Google at myaccount.google.com, and you can always write to sumshine.game@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
